Internet issues are usually resolved within 24 hours.

Water leakage and plumbing emergencies are considered high priority and should be escalated immediately.

Electrical hazards such as smoke, sparks, or exposed wires require urgent maintenance attention.

Mess food complaints should be reviewed by hostel management within 12 hours.

Cleanliness complaints are assigned to housekeeping staff.

Security-related complaints must be escalated to hostel administration immediately.
